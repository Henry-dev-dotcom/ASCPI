Immunology traps test whether you know which part of the immune system does what, and whether a test is a screen or a confirmation. Each listed trap is explained below, followed by more classics.

Trap 1: "IgG comes first"

IgG is the most abundant antibody, which tempts people to pick it. But IgM is made first in a new (primary) response, and as a big pentamer with ten binding sites it is the best at activating complement and at agglutinating. IgG dominates the later, memory response. So IgM = recent infection; IgG alone = past infection or immunity.

Trap 2: "all hypersensitivity is antibody-driven"

Types I, II and III involve antibodies (IgE; IgG/IgM against cells; immune complexes). Type IV involves T cells, not antibody. That's why it takes 48-72 hours (T cells have to travel to the site and recruit macrophages) and why it can't be passed on with serum. Examples: TB skin test, poison ivy, nickel allergy, graft rejection.

Trap 3: using the wrong syphilis test to monitor treatment

Treponemal tests (FTA-ABS, TP-PA, EIA) detect antibody against the organism and stay positive for life, even after a cure. Nontreponemal tests (RPR, VDRL) detect reagin, and their titer falls with successful treatment. Use the RPR titer to follow therapy; a fourfold fall means a response.

Trap 4: a positive ANA means lupus

The ANA is a sensitive screen: nearly all SLE patients are positive, but so are other autoimmune diseases, people on some drugs, and about 5% of healthy people. A positive ANA must be followed by specific tests. Anti-dsDNA and anti-Smith are specific for SLE. A negative ANA makes SLE very unlikely.

More immunology traps

ProzoneVery high antibody can give a false-negative agglutination or precipitation test (for example RPR in secondary syphilis). Dilute and repeat.
Heterophile in young childrenA negative Monospot in a child under about 4-5 doesn't rule out mono. Use EBV-specific antibodies.
IgA deficiency and celiac testingAn IgA-based tTG test is falsely negative if the patient is IgA deficient. Check total IgA.
Hepatitis B vaccinationVaccinated people have anti-HBs only, never anti-HBc. Anti-HBc means a real infection at some time.
BCG and the skin testBCG vaccination can cause a positive tuberculin skin test. IGRAs are not affected.
Complement handlingHeating serum to 56 °C for 30 minutes destroys complement. Samples for complement tests must be fresh or frozen.
Rheumatoid factorRF is not specific for rheumatoid arthritis. Anti-CCP is.

How to approach an immunology trap

  1. Ask which arm of the immune system is involved: antibody (which class?), T cell or complement.
  2. Ask whether the test is a screen (sensitive) or a confirmation (specific).
  3. Think about timing: IgM vs IgG, window periods, and which markers stay for life.

Check your understanding

Which antibody class best activates complement?

IgM.

After syphilis treatment, which test should fall in titer?

The nontreponemal test (RPR or VDRL).

A reaction appears 48 hours after skin contact with nickel. Which hypersensitivity type, and what mediates it?

Type IV, mediated by T cells (no antibody).
